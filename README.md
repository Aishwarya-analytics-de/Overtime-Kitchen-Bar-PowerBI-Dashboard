# Overtime Kitchen & Bar - Power BI Dashboard

# Project Title

This Power BI solution provides restaurant management with a comprehensive view of sales performance, customer behavior, operational efficiency, and key business KPIs.

## Business Question
Restaurant managers require timely reporting to monitor sales trends, peak operating periods, customer activity, and overall business performance.

## Data
Source, number of rows, time period.

## Approach
Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard enabling stakeholders to:

- Monitor daily and monthly sales
- Analyze customer behavior
- Identify top-performing products
- Track operational KPIs
- Support data-driven decision making

## Key Findings

Overall performance
- Revenue per branch averaged €741.87K and revenue per employee €38.64K.

Branch performance
- Nightlife District leads with €1.19M, about 32% of total revenue and roughly 3x Tourist Biergarten (€0.38M).

Peak times
- 12:00-12:59 is the strongest hour (€580K), and 12:00-13:59 together bring in about 29% of revenue.
Saturday is the strongest day (€699K) and Sunday the weakest (€444K). Friday and Saturday together make up about 35% of revenue.

Weekday vs. weekend
- Downtown is a business-lunch location, with 91.5% of revenue on weekdays and only 8.5% on weekends.
Recreational Park (49.3%) and Tourist Biergarten (47.0%) earn almost half their revenue on weekends.
